# Methods and claim boundary

1. **Retrospective technology counterfactual.** Chronos-2 did not exist throughout the 2017–2026 window. The historical exercise inserts today's pretrained model into the historical F2R decision process; it is not evidence of contemporaneous live availability.
2. **Selection exposure.** The selected hybrid and comparison set were developed in the same broader historical research program. The evidence is therefore not an untouched holdout or a selection-adjusted confirmation.
3. **Information boundary.** The F2R-side price data available at each signal date are held fixed, but model representations and prior information are not identical. Chronos-2's pretraining corpus is not audited here.
4. **Attribution boundary.** The 99.4% changed-decision share is an exact accounting/transmission diagnostic. With equal-weight Top-4 portfolios, unchanged selections have the same gross holdings by construction; the result is not independent causal proof of model usefulness.
5. **Concentration.** The five largest favorable changed decisions are 35.2% of positive changed-decision contribution and 71.3% of the net relative log-wealth gap. Negative changed decisions offset a substantial part of gross favorable contribution.
6. **No optimum/adoption-policy claim.** The displayed weights are historical configurations. They do not identify a statistically optimal Chronos-2 weight or validate a general model-adoption policy.
