# Data and Rights

This repository contains author-generated replication code, derived research evidence, publication figures, metadata, and validation outputs associated with the working paper **A Second Opinion for the Portfolio: How a New Forecasting Model Changes Cross-Asset Allocation Decisions**.

## Included

- frozen derived model/portfolio evidence used by the manuscript;
- paper-level replication and validation code;
- derived decision-attribution evidence;
- publication figure PDFs for comparison; and
- the versioned working-paper PDF included as a reference snapshot.

## Not redistributed

The repository does not redistribute:

- downloaded raw market-price source files;
- proprietary or live production F2R configuration;
- credentials, APIs, private runtime state, or deployment artifacts;
- Chronos-2 model weights or third-party training data; or
- a complete model-refitting / live-trading implementation.

Chronos-2 and any third-party software or data remain subject to their own licenses and terms.

The derived evidence here is provided to support verification of the paper's reported results. It should not be interpreted as investment advice or as a live investment product.
